About Firestone

Global client service with a strong Middle East presence

Since 2007, Firestone Fire and Security Systems LLC has been a trusted provider of fire protection and security solutions across the UAE. With operational bases in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Al Ain, Sharjah and Ras Al Khaimah, we are dedicated to safeguarding lives and properties throughout all Emirates.

Our expertise covers fire and safety systems, fire alarms, fire sealants, gas safety systems, IT security solutions and ventilation systems — delivered end-to-end, from system design to installation and maintenance, to the highest industry standards.

What we do

Explore our specialised services

Six divisions, one accountable partner — engineered, installed and maintained to UAE Civil Defence standards.

01

Fire Protection Services

Design, supply and installation of fire alarm systems, smoke detection, fire fighting systems, sprinkler systems and FM200 gas suppression across Abu Dhabi and the UAE. ADCD approved — from AutoCAD shop drawings to testing, commissioning and Annual Maintenance Contracts.

02

Gas Systems

FM200 gas suppression, LPG and central gas system design, supply, installation and AMC in Abu Dhabi. DOE approved gas safety systems — fully compliant with UAE Civil Defence regulations.

03

Security & ELV Systems

Supply and installation of CCTV cameras, access control, gate barrier systems and audio-video intercom in Abu Dhabi. SIRA and MCC approved — including ADMCC approved CCTV Annual Maintenance Contracts.

04

Fire Sealant & Fire Stopping

Firestop sealants, firestop sprays and intumescent coating systems across the UAE. FCIA certified passive fire protection contractor — DOE approved penetration sealing for walls, floors and building joints.

05

Smoke Management Systems

DOE approved smoke management, smoke ventilation and exhaust system design, supply and installation — compliant with UAE Civil Defence and air ventilation regulations for commercial, industrial and residential buildings.

06

Civil Defence Compliance

ADCD fitness, Istifa, DCD and SCD certificate renewals, inspections, documentation and approvals. We manage your fire AMC, shop drawings and evacuation plan submissions so your facility passes every inspection.
